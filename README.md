# 🎯 Overview

PharmaInsight AI is a **full-stack sales intelligence platform built specifically around pharmaceutical distribution workflows**.

The application goes beyond basic CRUD operations by connecting transactional sales data with:

- Customer intelligence
- Product intelligence
- Revenue analytics
- Sales trends
- Business reports
- PDF generation
- Natural-language AI analysis

The central idea is:

```text
Raw Business Data
       ↓
     Sales
       ↓
   Analytics
       ↓
    Reports
       ↓
 AI-Powered Insights
       ↓
Better Business Decisions
